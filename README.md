## 🌍 About Me

I'm a climate and atmospheric scientist specializing in **data analysis, visualization, and modeling** of atmospheric phenomena. My work focuses on:

- 🔬 **Atmospheric Physics & Aerosol Science** - Understanding aerosol transport, optical properties, and climate impacts
- 📡 **Satellite & Reanalysis Data Processing** - Working with CAMS, ERA5, Sentinel-5P, and MERRA datasets
- 🌬️ **Trajectory Modeling** - HYSPLIT, FLEXPART, and atmospheric transport simulations
- 🌪️ **Climate & Weather Modeling** - WRF model post-processing and meteorological analysis
- 📊 **Data Visualization** - Creating publication-quality figures and interactive visualizations
