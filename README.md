## 🌍 About Me

I'm a **Research Associate** specializing in atmospheric science, emissions inventory assessment, and air quality modeling. With a PhD in Atmospheric Physics from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, I combine rigorous scientific training with practical programming expertise to solve complex environmental challenges.

### 🎯 Research Focus

- **Emissions Modeling & Inventory Analysis** - EPA MOVES, CARB EMFAC, ESTA, and emissions QA/QC for mobile and stationary sources
- **Atmospheric Composition & Transport** - Trace gas atmospheric rivers, aerosol transport, long-range pollution events
- **Satellite Remote Sensing** - CrIS, TROPESS, TROPOMI, MODIS data processing and atmospheric monitoring
- **Climate & Weather Modeling** - WRF-Chem simulations, reanalysis integration (ERA5, MERRA-2, CAMS)
- **Air Quality & Health Impacts** - PM₂.₅, aerosol radiative forcing, atmospheric heating, and climate-air quality feedbacks
- **Data Visualization & Analysis** - Publication-quality visualizations, scientific computing, and large-scale data management

### 🔬 Research Expertise

- Atmospheric aerosol physics and optical properties
- Black carbon transport and climate impacts
- Himalayan and Tibetan Plateau atmospheric dynamics
- Asian monsoon and biomass burning influences
- Machine learning applications in climate science
